# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to provide a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive
criticism, take responsibility for mistakes, and prioritize the health of the
community. Harassment, insults, trolling, sexualized attention, publishing
private information, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement and scope

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions and interactions that violate
this policy. Reports may be made privately through the repository owner's
GitHub profile and will be handled with appropriate confidentiality. This Code
applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
